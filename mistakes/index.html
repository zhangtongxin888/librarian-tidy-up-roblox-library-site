Guide desk/Mistakes

Common mistakes

Stop paying for uncertainty with extra walking.

Most beginner friction comes from changing focus too often or starting a trip before the destination is clear.

01

Picking random colors across the room

What it feels like: Every book creates a different trip.

Better move: Choose one vivid color and clear a small local route before switching.

02

Ignoring section labels

What it feels like: You wander near shelves hoping the destination becomes obvious.

Better move: Read the category and area code first; let labels narrow the search.

03

Splitting identical titles

What it feels like: Matching books become harder to recognize later.

Better move: Place identical titles together whenever you shelve them.

04

Refusing to use a hint

What it feels like: One uncertain book stalls the whole route.

Better move: Use a hint when the title-to-section match is unclear, then keep moving.

05

Treating 2,400 books as one marathon

What it feels like: The collection feels too large to start.

Better move: Work in focused sessions. The official description currently says progress saves.
