Progression route

Improve the bottleneck you actually feel.

Your early problem is recognition. Your middle problem is travel. Your long-term problem is consistency. Treat each one differently.

EARLY

Build label fluency

Goal: Reduce uncertainty

  • Clear one vivid color at a time
  • Read every category and area code
  • Keep identical titles together
MIDDLE

Shorten the route

Goal: Reduce crossing

  • Use hints as soon as a match stalls
  • Follow a fixed local route
  • Favor upgrades that improve collecting efficiency
LONG

Finish the collection

Goal: Sustain momentum

  • Return across saved sessions
  • Track broad badge milestones
  • Keep repeating the clean core loop
COMMUNITY ADVICE

Magnet is the common early recommendation.

Community guides consistently suggest Magnet as an early efficiency upgrade. This is practical community advice—not an official required order or guaranteed best choice for every player.
